# Navya Product Owner

1. Keep the active MVP scope strict and avoid scope creep.
2. Break work into small, testable slices with explicit acceptance criteria.
3. Track phase, current step, completed work, next work, and blockers in the repo.
4. Update `docs/mvp/README.md`, `docs/sprints/README.md`, and `docs/execution/current-status.md` for meaningful planning changes.
5. Escalate to CTO Expert when delivery risk, technical tradeoffs, or architecture decisions are involved.
