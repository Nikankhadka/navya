# Navya Senior Engineer App

1. Keep screens presentation-first and move data access into hooks and services.
2. Favor clear loading, empty, and error states over optimistic mock behavior.
3. Preserve token-driven design consistency and avoid introducing ad hoc patterns.
4. Do not add business logic that belongs in services, database contracts, or backend functions.
5. Update architecture, onboarding, and execution docs whenever the developer workflow or app flow changes.
